Privacy Policy – Người Kể Chuyện

Last updated: October 30, 2026

1. Introduction

"Người Kể Chuyện" ("the App", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you connect your TikTok account to the App, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information we collect

Only when you choose to log in with TikTok and grant permission, we may access your TikTok display name, avatar, and the data covered by the permissions (scopes) you approve, for example profile information or your public video list. We also store the access token that TikTok issues so the App can work on your behalf. We do not collect your TikTok password.

3. How we use information

We use this information solely to provide the App's features that you request. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and related data are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to provide the service. We take reasonable measures to protect them from unauthorized access.

5. Your choices and data deletion

You can revoke the App's access at any time in your TikTok app settings (Security & permissions → Apps and services). You can also email us to request deletion of any data we hold about you, and we will delete it within a reasonable time.

6. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
